# Character assets

Model, skins and the Idle/Run animations come from **Animated Characters 3** by Kenney
(https://www.kenney.nl), released under **CC0** (see LICENSE-kenney-cc0.txt). Credit is not
required but is given here.

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/animated-characters-3 (kenney_animated-characters-3.zip)
- `character.glb` is `Model/characterMedium.fbx` plus the Idle and Run clips, converted with
  `tools/convert-characters.mjs`. The skins are the unmodified 256x256 PNGs from the pack.
